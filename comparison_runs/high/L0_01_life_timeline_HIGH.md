## 8. Guardrails for using this module

1. **Do not treat this as biography.**  
   It is a thematic map built from limited evidence.

2. **Do not infer sequence from current coherence.**  
   Present-day integration may be retroactive narrative.

3. **Do not use personality profiles as life-history proof.**  
   MBTI, DISC, Big Five, and vocational data are clues, not chronology.

4. **Do not upgrade “interest” into “competence” without artifacts.**  
   Competence requires outputs, results, peer validation, or repeated performance.

5. **Do not frame every domain as noble or strategic.**  
   Politics, trading, systems, fatherhood, and PHOM may include mixed motives, constraints, avoidance, frustration, or unresolved needs.

6. **Do not preserve the trading narrative untested.**  
   “Good analysis, weak execution” must be tested against logs.

7. **Do not make “system-builder” the master explanation.**  
   Track contradictory states: impulsive, relational, avoidant, playful, exhausted, simple, practical, socially expansive.

8. **Use this module operationally.**  
   When a new life event is added, tag it with:
   - date/period  
   - domain  
   - source  
   - before pattern  
   - after pattern  
   - behavioral evidence  
   - counter-evidence
