   Children may be deeply important while actual time and routines are limited by circumstances.

   It may be a local intervention or campaign experiment rather than durable public-role transformation.

## 10. Practical use of this module

1. **Do not treat this as a biography yet.**  
   It is a structured evidence map for building a future biography.

2. **Do not infer childhood from current traits.**  
   Childhood and adolescence remain mostly unknown.

3. **Separate central value from central behavior.**  
   Example: “children are a stated priority” is safer than “fatherhood organizes the whole life” until behavior confirms it.

4. **Separate project from life phase.**  
   QI, WHER, politics, trading, and PHOM become life phases only if they dominate time, decisions, identity, sacrifices, and outcomes over sustained periods.

5. **Require behavioral cost for identity-shift claims.**  
   A real shift should show changes in time, money, relationships, public commitments, outputs, or abandoned alternatives.

6. **Keep falsification active.**  
   Future evidence should be allowed to show that system-building is recent, fatherhood is constrained, politics is temporary, trading issues are strategy-related, or mission language is aspirational.

7. **State dependence is deferred.**  
   Some patterns may depend heavily on stress, fatigue, flow, financial pressure, or public exposure, but that belongs in a later PHOM layer unless linked to dated life events.
